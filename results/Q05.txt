Q05: patch와 작성자를 연결해 누가 어떤 patch를 만들었는지 확인한다. [INNER JOIN]

SQL:
SELECT p.id, p.title, d.name AS author, p.status
FROM patches AS p
INNER JOIN developers AS d ON d.id = p.developer_id
ORDER BY p.id;
