Q06: test run과 patch를 연결해 실패한 test의 patch 제목을 확인한다. [INNER JOIN]

SQL:
SELECT t.id, p.title, t.device, t.suite, t.failed
FROM test_runs AS t
INNER JOIN patches AS p ON p.id = t.patch_id
WHERE t.failed > 0
ORDER BY t.failed DESC, t.id;
