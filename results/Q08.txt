Q08: review와 patch/reviewer를 연결해 review 이력을 확인한다. [INNER JOIN]

SQL:
SELECT r.id, p.title, d.name AS reviewer, r.verdict
FROM reviews AS r
INNER JOIN patches AS p ON p.id = r.patch_id
INNER JOIN developers AS d ON d.id = r.reviewer_id
ORDER BY r.id;
