Q10: 장치별 평균 test 실행 시간을 구한다. [집계: AVG, GROUP BY]

SQL:
SELECT device, ROUND(AVG(duration_sec), 2) AS avg_duration_sec
FROM test_runs
GROUP BY device
ORDER BY device;

RESULT:
device | avg_duration_sec
-------+-----------------
RPi4   | 53.95           
RPi5   | 60.51           
x86    | 68.1
