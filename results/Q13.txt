Q13: review가 아직 하나도 없는 patch를 찾는다. [서브쿼리: NOT EXISTS]

SQL:
SELECT p.id, p.title
FROM patches AS p
WHERE NOT EXISTS (
    SELECT 1
    FROM reviews AS r
    WHERE r.patch_id = p.id
)
ORDER BY p.id;

RESULT:
id | title
