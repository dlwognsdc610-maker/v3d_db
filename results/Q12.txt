Q12: 평균보다 변경 줄 수가 큰 patch를 찾는다. [서브쿼리]

SQL:
SELECT id, title, lines_changed
FROM patches
WHERE lines_changed > (SELECT AVG(lines_changed) FROM patches)
ORDER BY lines_changed DESC;
