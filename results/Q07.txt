Q07: patch가 없는 개발자도 포함해 개발자별 patch 개수를 확인한다. [LEFT JOIN]

SQL:
SELECT d.name, COUNT(p.id) AS patch_count
FROM developers AS d
LEFT JOIN patches AS p ON p.developer_id = d.id
GROUP BY d.id, d.name
ORDER BY d.id;
